# AI model roadmap

## Phase 10 · 18 November 2026

Two optional Gemini Flash operations extend the existing recipe generation service. Weekly planning selects seven curated recipe IDs through validated structured output. The shopping assistant can ask Gemini to select an enumerated intent and renders the answer using the authenticated CartIQ service. The model cannot return a merchant price as an assistant tool result. Pantry names are included in recipe requests only when selected. The existing rules and genuine-observation forecasting eligibility checks remain available.

CartIQ uses the Gemini REST generateContent endpoint through HTTPX. A Google Gemini SDK is not used here. The published stable identifier `gemini-3.5-flash` supports generateContent. Availability for this account requires a separate authorized verification. No foundation model was trained, forecast recalibrated or production accuracy estimated in this phase. [Google model and API support](https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/docs/whats-new-gemini-3.5)

|---|---|---|

Recipe discovery and planning also run independently from Gemini. | Authorized live provider check, human review for recipe quality, India-language requests and units, and boundary cases for unsupported nutrition and health claims. |

No genuine model has been trained. | Authorized training and derived-use grants, enough recent same-day-ingested history by exact listing, variant and location, and reported real holdout results. Seasonal and tree experiments require a stronger genuine baseline. |
| Budget and shopping recommendations | Phase 4 adds deterministic exact-pack advice, budget-aware basket search, curated pantry recipe ranking and explicit account-scoped suggestions. | Independently reviewed local retailer, stock and fee evidence plus authorized shopper outcomes for ranking or real-world utility evaluation. |

No new model was trained in Phase 2. No nutritional information is generated. A later nutrition feature needs a licensed and reliable food-composition source, documented preparation state and serving conversion, and validation with missing values preserved.

Phase 4 trained no model. Evaluate deterministic decisions first against reviewed cases for equivalent packs, spoilage risk, unavailable essentials, fee and minimum-order effects, and incomplete data. Use synthetic cases only for regression. Any learned ranker needs a permission-approved dataset, account-aware train/test separation and a comparison with the deterministic baseline. ## Unified recommender evaluation contracts

`ai-service/recommendation_contracts.py` defines independent versioned feature and label contracts and checks an offline JSON manifest. Supply `schema: 1`, task, version, rows and two ISO cutoff dates for chronological tasks. Each row needs a digest, split, observation date, feature keys, explicit label, de-identified grouping digests, evidence reference and currently valid reviewed training and evaluation grants. Unknown feature values use null. No legacy simulated price history or unlicensed records are eligible. The tool checks supplied authorization metadata. An authorized reviewer must verify the underlying evidence and permissions.

Run `python -B recommendation_contracts.py <manifest.json> --as-of YYYY-MM-DD` from `ai-service`. Output is a dataset checksum, split/label counts and coverage state. A coverage result does not authorize training or production serving. Manifests remain restricted and should use opaque reviewer-issued identifiers for grouped source records, both product families in a pair where relevant, and pseudonymous account cohorts. Keep the test manifest sealed during development. Where chronological and group rules conflict, exclude boundary-spanning groups or construct independent cohorts eligible for one window. Fit preprocessing only to training records and separate explicit negative labels from unknowns and non-response.

| Contract | Features and reviewed labels | Baseline and measures | Evidence required before release |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `product_matching` | Brand, title, barcode, pack, flavour and formulation on both listings. Labels are exact same variant, distinct, or unresolved, with evidence for each listing. | Existing deterministic scorer and exact-barcode baseline. Report precision, recall, F1, false-match rate and manual review coverage. Group by source records and canonical families across train, validation and test. | Independent human adjudication and a locked evaluation set with enough genuine missing/conflicting barcode and pack/flavour/formulation cases to bound false matches by slice. |
| `recipe_recommendation` | Explicit diet, cuisine, servings, known pantry coverage and curated recipe identity. Labels identify intentionally selected, rejected, irrelevant or unresolved suggestions. | Current curated pantry-coverage ranker. Report NDCG@5, Recall@5, dietary-constraint violations and missing-ingredient burden. Use de-identified account, recipe/product-family and exposure groups across chronological splits. | Authorized recipe and exposure records plus independent dietary and pantry validation. Require improvement on the locked holdout and no confirmed dietary-constraint violations before serving. |
| `bulk_buy` | Exact pack and unit price, stock, consumption, household size, pantry, storage, use-by, budget, retailer and location. Reviewed labels identify normal, larger, multiple, no extra or insufficient data. | Current deterministic rules. Report unsafe extra-purchase rate, action agreement, coverage on complete evidence and abstention quality. Group account, pack family and offer snapshots across chronological windows. | Permission-approved stock, use-by and household constraint evidence and independent review of all high-risk recommendations. Require a defensible safety bound by cohort before serving. |
| `personalized` | Saved and previously selected packs, explicit diet, pantry gap, budget and fresh retailer/location offer status. Labels are accepted, rejected, modified, irrelevant or unresolved after exposure. | Current explicit-signal ranking. Measure NDCG@5, coverage, budget or diet violations and acceptance/modification by cohort. Group account, product family and exposure records across chronological windows. | Opt-in training and evaluation rights, independently reviewed exposure data with enough labelled outcomes across cohorts, and benchmark improvement without added violations. |

Freeze sample sizes, uncertainty bounds and minimum improvements before opening each held-out test set. The admin feedback counts help identify rule areas to investigate. Self-reports alone do not establish prediction accuracy or purchase outcomes. Phase 5 collected no training dataset, trained no model and changed no deployed policy weights.
